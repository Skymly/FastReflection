# The 1.0 surface is the union of the four reference libraries, pruned by council verdict

The 1.0 surface of `Skymly.FastReflection` is the union of the public capabilities of fast-member, Dynamitey, Namotion.Reflection and DotNext.Reflection, pruned by the council verdicts recorded on tickets [#7](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/7) and [#11](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/11)–[#19](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/19) and indexed row-by-row in [`docs/plans/1.0.md`](../plans/1.0.md). The union is the candidate pool, not the surface: every candidate carries a verdict — In, Satellite, post-1.0, or wontfix — decided on its ticket. This ADR records the rules the council applied and the resulting shape; it transcribes and organizes, it does not re-decide.

## Pruning rules

The verdicts were produced by applying, in order:

- **The ADR-0002 noun-admission test.** A capability is In only when its contract is true in the existing glossary language; a live reference return fails it (field `ref` access, [#17](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/17)), and no ticket admitted a noun. A capability that cannot be In may still be Satellite, post-1.0, or wontfix.
- **Census call-site evidence.** The demand bar is production call sites in the [#2](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/2) census of 52 dependents across the four reference libraries. Zero-external-use items default to wontfix where a BCL composition covers them (`IsDBNull`, `GetCommonBaseType`, `GenericDelegateType` — [#14](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/14), [#16](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/16)) and to post-1.0 where the capability stays expressible but unproven (member paths, events by name — [#13](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/13)).
- **Post-1.0 beats Satellite** unless the satellite carries the full quality gates cheaply — [#12](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/12) tested and rejected post-1.0 for `ObjectReader` on gate cost. The two satellites, `ObjectReader` ([#12](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/12)) and `XmlDocs` ([#15](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/15)), sit outside the core under the ADR-0002 satellite rules and [#19](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/19)'s topology.
- **The AOT policy and #7's wontfix set pre-decide the dynamic domain.** `IDynamicMetaObjectProvider`/`DynamicObject` targets, COM interop, DLR callsite machinery, user-defined conversions, generic type inference, const narrowing, and any `Microsoft.CSharp`/DLR dependency are wontfix wherever they appear ([#7](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/7) Q7, [#9](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/9); applied in [#11](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/11) Q1, [#14](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/14), [#16](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/16), [#18](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/18)).
- **Receiver and namespace shape bar items outright.** `this object`/`string`/`Delegate`/`MemberInfo` extensions and `Type` extensions beyond `CreateFactory` fail ADR-0002's shape budget regardless of demand (`FastDynamicInvoke`, `Dynamic.Invoke`, `HasProperty`/`TryGetPropertyValue`, the DotNext `Type`/`MethodBase` extensions — [#14](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/14), [#15](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/15), [#16](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/16), [#17](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/17)).

## Verdict summary

- **In (core):** the shipped 0.1 surface; name-based member binding — invoke/get/set/construct by name, target-free statics, indexers, bounded `InvokeConvert`, `GetMemberNames`, and the bound-invoker cache ([#7](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/7), [#13](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/13)); member attribute reads, `GetMembers()` + caller-side selection, explicit ordinal ordering, and the `TryGetOrdinal`/`CreateNewSupported` probes ([#11](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/11)).
- **Satellite:** `Skymly.FastReflection.ObjectReader` — the `IEnumerable`→`DbDataReader` adapter with name-list columns, ordinal ordering, and the `SqlBulkCopy`/`DataTable.Load` surface ([#12](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/12)); `Skymly.FastReflection.XmlDocs` — XML-doc reading on `MemberInfo`/`Type`/`ParameterInfo`/`Assembly` ([#15](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/15)). Package ids, namespaces, TFM parity and lockstep versioning per [#19](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/19).
- **Post-1.0:** member paths and events by name ([#13](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/13)); `DbColumn`/`GetColumnSchema` metadata ([#12](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/12)); the doc-tooling domain — the `ContextualType` carrier and contextual member types, the attribute split, NRT reading ([#15](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/15)) and string-name type checks ([#16](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/16)); the companion source-generator package — `UnsafeAccessor` externs, typed accessors, `KeyOf<T>`, AOT/trim carriers ([#18](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/18)).
- **Wontfix:** the dynamic domain ([#7](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/7) Q7, [#11](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/11) Q1, [#14](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/14)); `ObjectReader`'s ctor, reverse direction, bulk helpers, non-public columns and probes ([#12](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/12)); the nullability-validation trio, `GetXmlDocsPath`/`ToXmlDocsContent`/`GetXmlDocsRecordPropertySummary`, public cache flushes, the Cecil readers, `IPropertyReader`/`IPropertyWriter`, `GetEnumerableItemType` ([#15](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/15)); the type-utility sweep ([#16](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/16)); the `Type<T>` lookup/member-object/argument-pack/closed-bind/`Dynamic.Invoke`/`MemberOf`/custom-exception/`ExtensionRegistry` sweep ([#17](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/17)); `UnsafeAccessor` as the core mechanism ([#18](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/18)); statics-as-Members, `GetMembersSupported`, `MemberSet`, declaration-order, case-insensitive names and the FastMember source shim ([#11](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/11)); operator verbs and `CoerceConvert` ([#13](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/13)).

## Considered options

- **Verbatim fast-member parity.** Rejected: the used surface already mirrors it and the deltas are semantics — the [#8](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/8) exception contract, `IReadOnlyList<Member>` over `MemberSet`, `MemberInfo` over `GetAttribute` spellings — that a `FastMember` source shim would silently mismatch; README migration tables serve the migrant instead ([#11](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/11) Q8).
- **Adopt the whole union.** Rejected: it recreates four libraries inside one glossary — the failure the concept model exists to prevent ([#6](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/6), ADR-0002 considered options) — and ships zero-demand surface the census could not evidence ([#2](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/2)).
- **Core-only, no satellites.** Rejected: `ObjectReader`'s `SqlBulkCopy` scenario and Namotion's XML-doc reads are the two broadest external demands in the census ([#2](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/2)), and their dependency domains — ADO `DbDataReader`, doc-file IO — are exactly what the ADR-0002 satellite boundary exists to hold ([#12](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/12) Q1, [#15](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/15)).

## Consequences

- The per-member spellings are [`docs/specs/1.0-public-api.md`](../specs/1.0-public-api.md) ([#20](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/20)); the gate list is [`docs/specs/1.0-quality-gates.md`](../specs/1.0-quality-gates.md) ([#21](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/21)); the per-candidate ledger is `docs/plans/1.0.md`, which becomes the map's authoritative definition.
- `CONTEXT.md` keeps its nouns unchanged — no ticket admitted one — and its scope sentence narrows to the wontfix/post-1.0 set in this hop; the README non-goals follow it.
- Migration tables from each reference library, per-package READMEs, and the benchmark comparison rows land with [#20](https://github.com/Skymly/FastReflection/issues/20)'s implementation slices; the acceptance checklist lives in the plan.
- A verdict found wrong later is a maintainer override of the deciding ticket, not an edit to this record.
